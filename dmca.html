DMCA Policy

Last updated: July 2026

World Insight respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects its contributors to do the same. We publish original content and take copyright seriously.

Filing a Notice

If you believe content published on this Site infringes your copyright, please send a written notice through our Contact page including:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed
  • The exact URL on World Insight where the allegedly infringing material is located
  • Your name, address, phone number, and email address
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
  • A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on their behalf

Our Response

Upon receiving a valid notice, we will review the claim promptly and remove or disable access to the material if the claim is substantiated, in accordance with applicable copyright law.

Counter-Notices

If you believe material was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notice through the same contact channel, and we will review it accordingly.
